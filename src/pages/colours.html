Colour tips

Choosing colours with confidence

Check the light

Rooms facing north look cooler – warm creams and yellows balance them. Sunny rooms can carry cool blues and greens.

Use the 60-30-10 rule

60% main wall colour, 30% a supporting shade (furniture, curtains), 10% a bold accent like a feature wall or door.

Try before you commit

Paint a 2×2 ft patch and look at it in the morning and at night. Shades look different under tube lights and sunlight.
